# Training Program Policies

## Registration

Participants must register before 5:00 PM on 14 October. A valid identity document is required. Registration after the deadline requires written approval from the program coordinator.

## Attendance

Participants must attend at least 80% of the scheduled sessions to receive a completion certificate.

## Project Submission

Final projects must be submitted through the local competition portal before 3:30 PM. Email submissions are not accepted.
